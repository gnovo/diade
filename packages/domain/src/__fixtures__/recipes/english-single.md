# Spaghetti carbonara
servings: 4
tags: pasta, quick

A Roman classic. No cream, ever.

## Ingredients
- 400 g spaghetti
- 200 g guanciale
- 4 eggs
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup grated pecorino
- 1 lb beef mince
- black pepper to taste

## Steps
1. Boil the pasta in salted water.
2. Fry the guanciale until crisp.
3. Toss everything off the heat.
